Understanding the challenge

Flooding is a citywide
resilience issue.

Heavy rainfall can overwhelm urban drainage and leave residents dealing with flooded streets, interrupted travel and damage to property. Communities need clear information, responsive maintenance and long-term infrastructure planning.

Why act

Residents need answers and lasting solutions.

A flooded street is more than an inconvenience. It can make everyday journeys difficult, create risks for pedestrians and drivers, and affect homes and local businesses. Repeated flooding also leaves residents unsure where to report problems or when repairs will happen.

The Dry Streets Baku campaign calls for a coordinated response: investigate locations that flood repeatedly, assess the capacity and condition of drainage pipes, prioritize improvements, and communicate progress to the public.

Local knowledge matters. Residents can help identify recurring flood locations. Add only verified place names, dates and reports to this page before publishing.
